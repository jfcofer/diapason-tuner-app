## Tried and abandoned
- **§3's filter as written** (`beta` 0.02, `d_cutoff` 1 Hz, no bound): needle up to 18.8 ms past
  the ledger. An onset converges from as far as 45 ¢ while the window fills, and does not step.
  The median spends the two reserved hops on that ramp, and the filter at its rest cutoff trails it.
- **A faster `d_cutoff`** (5, 10, 30 Hz, `beta` unchanged): still 2.3–4.8 ms over.
- **A faster `beta`** (0.1–1): it meets the line from about `beta` 0.3 / `d_cutoff` 10, but held
  notes at 10 dB SNR wobble 2–6.4 ¢ (the spec's filter: 0.70 ¢).
- **Re-seeding the filter at the median past a threshold:** it passes both only at 4–5 ¢
  (3 ¢: 6.5 ¢ of wobble from re-seeding on noise; 6 ¢: 8 ms late), and it jumps the needle.
- **The bound** (clamp the output to within S of the median, keep the speed): it passes both for
  S = 3–4 ¢, and 4.5 ¢ is 3 ms late. 3 ¢ was taken for the latency margin.
- **Resetting on any octave change, or on one frame of another note** (the first draft): flicker on
  recordings. Replaced by an octave shift and a second median that must reach three frames.
- **Gating flicker only inside `recorded.rs`'s windows:** zero under the old rule too; the flicker
  is in the decays. The gate runs over the whole recording.

## Surprises
- The ledger's comment that the filter "adds nothing to the first movement" held only for a step
  from a reset. Fixed in `range::SMOOTHING_HOPS`'s doc.
- The reference 1€ filter takes its speed from its last *output*, so a lag inflates the speed: the
  steady lag on a ramp is a fixed point, not `v·τ(min_cutoff + β·v)`. My first unit test assumed the
  latter.
- The stopped and gap downward-change totals on the needle equal `T-003c`'s on the estimate; the
  12 dB-under ones fell by up to 485 ms, because the median removes the frames that wandered.
- **The review found what the synthetic tests could not:** on recordings, the guard settling a
  held note's octave reset the needle each time (`ukulele_e4`: 27 blank frames, all after the
  judged window, which `recorded.rs` did not read). Fixed by moving the history by the octave, and
  gated on whole recordings now. And "two hops" holds worst case against worst case, not run by run
  (up to 30 ms in Low): the docs said more than was true.
- The host was never quiet for `just bench` (load 40 right after `just verify`, then a JVM and
  Chrome). Untouched benches moved +68 % and +88 % between runs. No baseline saved.

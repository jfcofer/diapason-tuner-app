## Tried and abandoned
- **Gating ±0.5 ¢ at three periods of each tone.** I wrote that criterion, and it is stricter than
  the product. `AUDIO_ENGINE.md` §2 sizes the window to the lowest note *of the range*. The per-tone
  rule made the sine miss at −0.507 ¢ between grid points, and the property test flaked about once
  in 160 runs. The gate now uses §2's windows, and the stress sweep is recorded under ceilings.
- **Five interpolators to fix the full-band sawtooth:** spectral interpolation, bias correction, a
  lag-0 template, windowed sinc, and a pre-NSDF low-pass. Each one broke the sine or fell short.
  The bias comes from content near the analysis Nyquist (`T-003a` notes), so the cure belongs to
  `T-003b`'s filter.
- **f32 FFTs.** They are 25–29 % faster, but 20 times less accurate at 30–60 Hz, and their output
  depends on the input gain.

## Surprises
- **A sawtooth's NSDF peak is not parabolic at any window:** all harmonics add to its curvature
  equally. The 0.5 ¢ budget for bright tones depends on how low the decimation filter cuts.
- **The grid hid a failure, and the reviewer's dense probe found it.** Accuracy claims now come
  from dense probes, not the test's own grid.
- **The `dsp` clippy list (the RT list) also applies to tests,** so `println!` and `prop_assert!`
  are unavailable there.
- **Bench noise was caused by agents running in parallel:** up to +20 % on identical code. Alone on
  the host, two runs agreed within 0.3 %.
- **Docs that were wrong, all fixed:**
  - `TESTING.md` named a baseline path the script did not use;
  - `ARCHITECTURE.md` said `dsp` "depends on nothing but core numeric crates";
  - `CI_RELEASE.md` planned a CI bench job, which a host-bound baseline cannot support.
